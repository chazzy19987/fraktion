FAQ

Good questions.

How big is a bin?

Each bin is a 102 L heavy-duty tote with a snap-on lid — roughly 70 × 50 × 40 cm. To keep our crew safe, please keep each bin under about 25 kg.

How fast can I get a bin back?

Pick any day from tomorrow onward (Monday to Saturday) and a morning, afternoon or evening window. We'll confirm the time and text you when the driver is on the way.

Do I need to be home?

No. Add access notes in your account — a side door, garage code or porch — and we'll leave bins there.

What can't I store?

Anything perishable, flammable or hazardous, anything alive, cash, firearms, or items of high value like jewellery. If you're unsure, ask us first.

Does anyone else's stuff go in my bin?

Never. Your bins are sealed, serial-numbered and only ever opened by you.

How do delivery credits work?

Each plan comes with yearly credits — 4 on Starter, 10 on Family, unlimited on Pro. One credit covers one delivery, however many bins are in it. Extra deliveries are $9.99. Pickups are always free.

Can I cancel?

Any time. We'll deliver your bins back and stop billing at the end of the month.
